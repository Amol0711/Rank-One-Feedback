Numerical methods and execution coverage
========================================

Response samples use direct scaling-and-squaring matrix exponentials and
singular values in metric coordinates. The four response grids contain 1607
samples in total. Their stored times are inputs, not newly located maxima.
The spectral and finite-gain calculations use the same fixed physical model.

Stationary evaluation uses a descending bracket in fast time, negative local
curvature and a simple leading singular value. The fixed-time gain partial
is evaluated with a 6-by-6 block exponential and crosschecked by a 3-by-3
spectral exponential with divided differences. The two precision runs retain
all 44 fixed metric/gain cases. The comparison case at 34359738368 is separate
from that grid. It does not replace or extend the 44-row data selection.

Linked directions use one simultaneous sign choice. The companion singular
vector is transported through the response matrix rather than signed
independently. Physical-coordinate errors use the declared quadratic metric.
Shifted responses are evaluated at their own stationary times. The six shifted
cases also retain unshifted-time control calculations.

The finite-gain search samples both ends of each interval, refines sampled
local peaks and compares endpoint and interior candidates. Two gain-grid
densities are checked. The high-precision calculation evaluates eight cases
at 80 and 110 digits, including joint stationary equations for one interior
candidate. Conditional local curvature is not a global optimality certificate.

Directed enclosures use mpmath interval arithmetic with a degree-26 Taylor
expansion, an explicit truncation remainder and interval scaling/squaring.
Floating-point singular values propose bounds; interval LDL checks validate
the associated inequalities. Rational witness seeds are normalized separately.
Every one of the 128 closed reciprocal-gain cells is enclosed for each case.
Every positive-time sample through the terminal contraction is evaluated.
Time zero has norm one and is included in the grid maximum. The intersample
factor and terminal contraction supply the all-time response comparison.

The independent replay uses only Python integers and exact fractions in its
arithmetic kernel. It recomputes all cells and sample inequalities on a
256-bit fixed grid with a degree-34 Taylor expansion. Recorded norm bounds
serve as proposals, not trusted solutions. This numerical verification does
not establish correctness of the Python or arithmetic implementations.

Three fixed cases are evaluated at both interval precisions. The normalized
incumbent is 6.5 and the cutoff is 10. The strict displayed bounds are 1.102
from above and 1.119 from below. The tighter decay requirement 59/20 uses the
same normalized objective and incumbent; its strict feasibility is checked
separately by exact rational evaluation of the fixed cubic coefficients.

Stored-record checking is distinct from numerical reproduction. The check
mode verifies schemas, finite coverage, outward serialization, scalar
arithmetic and supplied inputs. The intervals and integer-replay modes
recompute the matrix enclosures. The stationary, directions, comparison and
finite-gain modes recompute numerical searches or evaluations. Export copies
retained data and generates the selected-row product from its one policy.

The supplied comparison rates and their three residual multipliers are not
recomputed. Scalar boundary observations and the four-state reference-accuracy
data are retained inputs. No numerical command establishes new comparison
ranges, a certified global gain optimizer or a certified residual enclosure.

Primary value comparisons use 1e-55 relative tolerance with a 1e-100 scale
floor. Finite-gain comparisons use 1e-50 for nonzero quantities and a 1e-60
absolute tolerance for numerically zero gain partials. Recomputed discrepancies
from stored decimal strings use 1e-45 relative tolerance to allow subtraction
loss. Floating-point response samples use 2e-10 times max(1, absolute value).
Matched-precision producer checks and directed strict inequalities remain
separate from these comparison tolerances.
